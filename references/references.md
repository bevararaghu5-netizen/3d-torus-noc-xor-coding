## Attribution and reproducibility

The RTL and testbench files in this repository were transcribed from the project's supplied `codes.pdf`. Verify their syntax and behavior in Vivado before relying on them. Figures and performance values are included as project-provided evidence; consult the thesis and record the experiment configuration before making general performance claims.

Only redistribute third-party papers when their license or the rights holder permits it. Prefer DOI/publisher links for papers that are not openly licensed.
